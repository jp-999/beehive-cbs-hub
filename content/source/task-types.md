## 🔀 Two task types: check before you start

Tasks now come in two types. Check the **Scenario Details** step of your task to see which one you have.

**Comparison tasks (the original ones).** You chat with **3 models** (A, B and C), rate each one, and rank them. Run Gemini at **gemini.google.com** on web, or the **Gemini app** on mobile (turn auto model selection off first).

**Single-model tasks (Google AI Mode, Gemini 4 Hydrogen).** The Scenario Details step shows **🤖 MODEL — GOOGLE AI MODE, GEMINI 4 HYDROGEN**. You chat with **one model only**, so you rate one model and there is **no ranking step**. The setup is different:

- Run the whole conversation at **[google.com/ai](https://google.com/ai)** (web) or the **Google app** (mobile). **Do not** use the Gemini app or gemini.google.com for this type.
- Sign in with your **demo account** and select **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** before Turn 1 (there is no Task mode, just pick the model).
- Copy the share link from **Share in AI Mode** (it looks like `share.google/aimode/…`), not the address bar.
- **No trajectory JSON.** Export a **PDF** instead (tick "Show original chat link" and "Include thinking content") and name it `trajectory_a.pdf`.
- Take the **Model Check screenshot** before Turn 1, with **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** and **AI Mode** visible.

Full step-by-step is in the Instructions (Materials page), section 2, "Reading your task row".
