**What it is** — a self-service page to claim your demo Google account, the one with access to the custom Gemini model you'll evaluate.

**How to claim**

1. Go to **[app.outlier.ai/demo-account-claim](https://app.outlier.ai/demo-account-claim)**.

**Keep in mind**

- **Gemini runs only on this account** — your personal Gmail doesn't have the custom model.
- Already claimed a Gemini demo account for **Nectarbloom or Power**? Keep using it — **don't claim a second one**, and don't use, share or swap any other account.
- **Start a new chat for every task**, and **never delete a chat** — yours or anyone else's.
- Mobile task? Chat in the **Gemini app**, then export it from your computer with **AI Exporter**.

> 🚫 **If your account doesn't work** — or you see "not eligible" / hit the model limit — **stop and ask in the War Room with your Task ID** before you start the conversation.

## 📣 Gemini mobile: turn off auto model selection

On the Gemini mobile app, Gemini sometimes switches from the required model to another model in the middle of a chat. **A run on the wrong model is not valid.**

**Fix: do this before Turn 1**

1. Open the **Gemini mobile app**.
2. Tap the **Menu** (top left).
3. Go to **Settings > Usage limits**.
4. Toggle **auto model selection off**.

**Notes**

- This is for **mobile only**.
- Check that the **correct model is still selected** before you send Turn 1.

> 📣 Source: [Gemini Mobile: Turn Off Auto Model Selection](https://community.outlier.ai/t/gemini-mobile-turn-off-auto-model-selection/2982084) (Outlier Community)

Check the **Scenario Details** step of your task before you start.

- **Comparison task (3 models):** run Gemini at **gemini.google.com** (web) or the **Gemini app** (mobile), with auto model selection turned off.

- **Only the last message showing?** Turn on **search history**.
- Select **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** and capture the Model Check screenshot with **AI Mode** visible before Turn 1.

### Reimbursements

Every purchase for this project must use the **credit card assigned to you in Outlier**. The **reimbursement form is only for one exceptional case**: you had a real problem with your assigned card and couldn't pay with it (for example, the payment didn't go through). **Only then** may you use your own card.

Submit the form with **(1)** screenshots of your receipt(s) and **(2)** a screenshot of the issue with the assigned card, and you'll be reimbursed.

- **Purchases on a personal card without a documented card issue are not reimbursed.**
