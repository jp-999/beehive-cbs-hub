## 🔄 Claim a replacement demo account

**New:** if your demo account isn't working, you can claim a replacement yourself from the same **Your Demo Account** page. No need to wait.

Click **"Having problems with this account?"**, then fill in the **Report a problem** form: choose what you see, add a short error message, and click **Get a new account**.

![Your Demo Account page: the "Having problems with this account?" link that starts a replacement](media/replacement-account/having-problems.png)

![Report a problem with this account: choose what you see, add an error message, then Get a new account](media/replacement-account/report-form.png)

**How it works**

- **Max 2 replacements** per CB.
- **Every report is saved.**
- A new account is assigned **only if there's a free one in the pool**. If there isn't, you keep your current account.
- Replaced accounts are **retired and never reused**.

> 🚨 **Only for accounts that aren't working.** It is not a way to get a second account when yours works fine.
